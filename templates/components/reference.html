About this course

Have you ever desired to be a Systematic and Daily Bible reader, to have intimacy with God and finish the whole Bible often as a way of life? Consistent Bible Reading discipline, called CBR, is neither a talent nor a gift of the Holy Spirit; rather, it is a discipline that most people can only acquire through a structured habit-formation training. Commit yourself to this training for the next seven months, following the instructions and guidelines God has led us to develop, and get ready to enjoy reading the Bible naturally for the rest of your life.

Reference Card definition

Consistent Bible Reading (CBR)

CBR is the discipline of reading through the Bible day by day to receive instructions for each new day from the Lord while aiming to finish it within a set time.

Seven key disciplines
  1. Early-rising routine (ERT)
  2. Consistent Bible Reading (CBR)
  3. Scripture Memory Practice (SMP)
  4. Art of Scriptural Meditation
  5. Simple Bible Study Methods (PERSONS, OPEN)
  6. Regular prayer life
  7. Building a character of perseverance
Personal Encourager Groups (PEGs)

PEGs are small groups within the main CBR training cohort. They help trainees walk the Bible-reading journey through prayer, study questions, group encouragement, and memory recitation.

The course material calls for at least three PEG study meetings each month before the next lesson.

Bible Study Methods

The devotional and character-study methods used throughout the CBR programme.

F.I.D — Daily Devotional Journal

Write brief FID journal notes each day after your CBR reading. Earn 1 Meditation point per week when done daily.

F

Focus — Write the key verse or subject reference from your reading today.

I

Insight — Record the key spiritual insight or observation the Holy Spirit revealed to you.

D

Doing — Describe the specific practical action or obedience conviction you are prompted to do today.

Share your FID with your PE group at least once per week to earn the 1 Accountability point.

O.P.E.N — Consistent Bible Reading & Study (from Session 2)

A four-step study method introduced in Session 2. Gather accurate observations, discover biblical principles, connect them to experience, and identify the need or action God is prompting.

O

Observations — Gather accurate biblical information: facts, names, events, principles, parables, and intriguing details.

P

Principles — Obtaining godly principles to live by is easy. Explore these three ways to discover principles in the Bible:

  1. Look for direct statements in the Scriptures that illustrate reasons for blessings or consequences for negative actions.
  2. Investigate why God blessed or cursed individuals in the Bible. (Did they uphold or break certain godly principles?)
  3. Compare one Scriptural passage to another to understand or explain why certain events occurred.
E

Experience — Reflect prayerfully on the facts and principles to discern what God is saying about your current circumstances.

N

Need — Apply the truth or conviction God has impressed on you. Write down what God has prompted you to do. This will bring blessings and prosperity from the Lord (see Jos 1:8; John 13:17; James 1:25).

P.E.R.S.O.N.S — Character Study (from Session 4)

A person is described by his behaviour. To prepare for a lifetime of Bible reading, study at least 5 of the 9 suggested qualities below. First read and understand the PERSONS steps, then write brief answers to the 7 questions in preparation for group discussion.

P

Personal perspective — Self-assessment of your understanding and what others say about this quality.
Q1. What does this quality mean to you when you reflect on or discuss it?

E

English meaning — Look up the definition of this quality in a dictionary or online.
Q2. How would you define or express this quality in your own words?

R

References — Study the provided Scriptures carefully to gain a spiritual understanding of the quality you have chosen.
Q3. Identify at least three biblical insights from your studied texts that add spiritual perspective to your knowledge.

S

Satan's opposing qualities — Based on your explored references, identify as many specific or implied negative attributes as possible that counteract the attribute you examined.
Q4. In what ways do some of these negative qualities obscure or detract from the quality you are investigating?

O

Obedience — Based on your new findings, describe practical ways this trait can be demonstrated in the life of a Christian.
Q5. How would you demonstrate this quality at home, in church, or the village, and how would it bring glory to God?

N

Note — Identify several ways to apply insights gained about this quality more specifically to maintain a consistent Bible reading lifestyle.
Q6. How can you apply this character quality to motivate you to keep CBR discipline for life?

S

Stirring others — Applying godly character traits makes a person very transformative for Christ.
Q7. Which of your Bible reading friends would you like to testify how to apply this quality to help them keep this priceless discipline of CBR?

🙏 Praying Back Scripture:

Praying back Scripture involves petitioning God based on what He has said in His Word. The Lord listens to us when we pray Scriptures because the Word is His will (1 Jn 5:14), and He watches over His Word to perform it. As you pray, the Holy Spirit may bring new thoughts to your mind, clarifying biblical observations into profound insights, just as He did with Daniel in Dan 9:2 and Dan 9:22. Adopt praying back the truths you discover during CBR devotions or Bible studies as a new spiritual discipline.

📿 Praying Back Psalm 119:

This is the longest chapter in the Bible — 176 verses divided into 22 sections of 8 verses each. Start by praying back 1 verse a day in Week 1, then increase by one verse each week, allowing this spiritual discipline to develop until you can pray back larger portions of Scripture with ease. Completing this Psalm earns 50 BONUS points.
